A diffusion policy that decides where vehicle tasks go can be steered to the attacker.

TG-Diff puts trust inside the denoising process of a diffusion offloading policy, and uses a digital twin at the roadside unit to expose nodes that lie about their queues. It targets collaborative V2V/V2I offloading with malicious roadside units, malicious helper vehicles, false state reports and poisoned training data.

Threat model

Four ways to attack a generative offloading policy

A1

Black-hole RSU

One roadside unit silently drops 60% of the tasks it receives.

A2

Malicious helpers

A share of helper vehicles drop 80% of the tasks offloaded to them over V2V.

A3

False state reports

Malicious nodes advertise an empty queue to attract traffic.

A4

Data poisoning

Part of the offline training data is relabelled to prefer malicious nodes.

Our systematic mapping of 59 studies found that 75% of generative-AI offloading studies ignore security and none protects the decisions of the generative model itself. TG-Diff addresses gaps G1 (secure generative decisions), G2 (V2V with trust) and G3 (decision time).

Method

Trust enters the policy twice

Trust from behaviour and from the twin

τⱼ = (sⱼ + 1) / (sⱼ + uⱼ + 2) · exp(−γ · |W̃ⱼ − Ŵⱼ|)

The beta reputation counts tasks returned on time (s) and lost (u). The twin term penalises a node whose reported backlog W̃ contradicts the backlog Ŵ predicted from the tasks the RSU dispatched, before any task is lost.

Trust-guided denoising

x̂₀,ᵢ ← x̂₀,ᵢ + λ · log τᵢ   at every DDIM step

The denoiser is trained with the DDPM objective by behaviour cloning from a privileged expert. Guidance acts inside the sampling trajectory, so an untrusted target is suppressed even if poisoned data taught the denoiser to prefer it.

Preliminary results · prototype, 3 evaluation seeds

What the prototype shows

1.5 km highway, 3 RSUs with MEC servers, 50 vehicles, 0.7 tasks per vehicle per second, 60 s episodes. Results will be replaced by the SUMO-based campaign.

Ablation

30% malicious, 20% poisoned
VariantOn timeLatencyLost

Trust guidance is the decisive component.

Value of the digital twin

liars only, 80 vehicles, heavy load
PolicyOn timeLatency

Nodes that lie about their queue but never drop tasks are invisible to behaviour-only trust; the twin exposes them.

Limits. Synthetic highway instead of SUMO traces; discrete target choice, where a trust-aware greedy heuristic is already strong (it also reaches 99% under attack, with lower latency than TG-Diff without attack); behaviour cloning instead of critic-based training; one training seed; non-adaptive attackers.
